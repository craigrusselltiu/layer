A little context

Outfits use feels-like temperatures, which account for wind and humidity. Day uses the daily high; Night uses the daily low. Your cold sensitivity and activity adjust the layers.

Dates within the next 16 days use a forecast where available. Later dates use averages for the same calendar days across the last five complete years, labelled Historical estimate. These are planning estimates, not a forecast.

The packing list includes day and night layers for every stop. Rain protection is added separately. The map colours show destination temperatures, not a continuous weather field.

Clothing is a starting point for everyday travel. Extreme cold, exposed terrain and specialist activities need specific preparation.

Trips stay in this browser. City searches and coordinates go to Open-Meteo; map tiles come from OpenStreetMap.
